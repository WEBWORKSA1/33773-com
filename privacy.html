Privacy & cookie policy

Effective October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information 33773.com ("we", "us") collects, how it is used and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (quote request, newsletter, contest entry, pledge, application or contact) we receive the details you type, such as your name, email, phone number, ZIP code and message. Form submissions are delivered to us through FormSubmit, a third-party form relay service, which processes them under its own privacy terms.

Quote requests are shared only with the licensed providers we match to your request, up to three, and only for the purpose of responding to it. We do not sell or rent your contact details to list brokers.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider and advertising partners receive standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages viewed and referring site. Our calculators and checklists store your inputs only in your own browser's local storage; that data is not sent to us.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message, and ads are served according to the choice made.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. Once played, YouTube may set cookies under Google's privacy policy. Images from NOAA load directly from noaa.gov.

Your choices and rights

  • You can unsubscribe from emails using the link in any message.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete information you submitted by choosing "Privacy request" on our contact form.
  • California residents may request disclosure of the categories of personal information collected and ask that it not be sold or shared; we do not sell personal information.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Contests are open only to people 18 or older.

Security and retention

The site is served over HTTPS. We keep form submissions only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable business records, then delete them.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the effective date above when we do.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the contact form and choose "Privacy request".
