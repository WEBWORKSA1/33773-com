Home / Local

The good stuff, ten minutes from home.

Most of mid-Pinellas's best free outdoor spaces are within a short drive of ZIP 33773. Hours below were checked in October 2026; confirm before you go.

  • Lake Seminole Park

    10015 Park Blvd N, Seminole

    258 acres on the lake with a boat ramp, a paved loop trail, 13 picnic shelters and some of the best sunrise views in the county. Open 7 a.m. to sunset.

  • Largo Central Park

    101 Central Park Dr, Largo

    Built on the old county fairgrounds and opened in 1995. Trails, a miniature railroad on scheduled days, events and the Largo Cultural Center. Open 5:30 a.m. to 10 p.m. The playground has been closed for construction since August 2026.

  • Florida Botanical Gardens

    12520 Ulmerton Rd, Largo

    150 acres of themed gardens and wetland boardwalks. Free entry, open daily 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. Ideal for ideas on wind-tolerant, Florida-friendly landscaping.

  • Heritage Village

    11909 125th St N, Largo

    A living-history museum of more than two dozen historic Pinellas buildings. Free, donations welcome. Wednesday to Saturday 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., Sunday 1 to 4 p.m.

  • Fred Marquis Pinellas Trail

    Countywide

    A paved multi-use trail running the length of the county on a former rail line, passing through Largo. Good for commuting by bike and for long walks.

  • Largo Cultural Center

    Inside Central Park

    Concerts, theatre and community events through the year.

City and county services

  • City of Largo: City Hall, utilities, permits and code enforcement. City Hall: 727-587-6700.
  • Pinellas County: parks, emergency management, solid waste and unincorporated-area services. Parts of 33773 are unincorporated county, so check which government serves your street.
  • Pinellas County Property Appraiser: values, exemptions and parcel lookups.
  • Pinellas County Tax Collector: property tax bills, driver licenses and vehicle tags.
